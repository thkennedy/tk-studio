# Convene the Council — the signature interaction (data asset, attended only)

Multi-perspective deliberation over the **installed agents already resolved
into this session's routes** (ST-5.2). The council is projection, not
population: every perspective is an installed resource speaking in turn, and
nothing about the deliberation persists — no roll call file, no council
memory, no state (AD-9).

## Who sits

Seats are drawn from the core's resolution, in this order:

1. Agents in the working set's routes (kind `agent`, user-authored included).
2. Agents shipped by working-set modules (e.g. bmm's analyst/pm/architect/dev,
   cis's coaches) — invoked by their stock names, untouched (AD-4).
3. The thin role wrappers (`developer`, `direction-giver`) when the question
   spans both postures.

If the resolution offers no agents at all, say so and deliberate as the
Studio alone — never invent a persona that has no installed resource behind
it.

## Form

1. **Frame** — restate the operator's question in one sentence; name the
   seats convened and why each earns its place for *this* question.
2. **Deliberate** — each seat speaks once, briefly, in its own register
   (the analyst probes, the architect constrains, the developer costs it).
   Disagreement is welcome; repetition is not. Two rounds maximum.
3. **Synthesize** — the Studio closes: the decision, the dissent worth
   recording, the next concrete step routed by name to a working-set
   resource.

## Boundaries

- Deliberation output lands wherever the operator's next step lands
  (a planning artifact through the adapter, kb note, or nothing) — the
  convening itself produces no artifact of its own and writes no state.
- Headless runs never convene: the same request headless goes straight to
  the routed resource with identical routing (the ST-5.2 comparison test
  holds this line). Presentation is the only difference.
