As the owner,
I want the supervisor to answer the figures per story, per stage and over time, the changes, and the before/after comparison with its verdict in words, over routes my browser and my tools can call,
So that every page and every script reads one computed answer and the rule for "faster, slower or no change" lives in one place (addendum §3.5; research §7).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Given** the store of Story 40.1, the route pattern of `api.ts:1141-1265` (`route()`, `fail`, `json`, `allow`), the cookie's read list (`api.ts:1097-1111` `cookieRoute`) and the CSRF rule (`:1104-1108`, `403 CsrfRefused`)
**When** Story 40.2 lands
**Then** `GET /analytics/summary?project=&from=&to=&band=` answers `{ok, project, range, tiles: {landed, cycle_median_s, cost_median_usd, first_leg_green_share, spend_usd}, previous: {…the same for the range before}, stages: [{stage, n, median_s, min_s, max_s, median_usd, median_tokens_in, median_tokens_out}], recent: [story rows]}`; `GET /analytics/stories?project=&from=&to=&band=&epic=` the `stories` rows in the range with each band's `{n, median, q1, q3, min, max}` for cost, cycle and hands-on; `GET /analytics/stories/{id}` one story with its `stages`, `legs`, `runs` and the band figures; `GET /analytics/stages?project=&stage=&from=&to=&band=` the per-story points of a stage with `rolling_median_5`; `GET /analytics/changes?project=` the `changes` rows newest first; `GET /analytics/export.csv?project=&table=` the named table as CSV (`text/csv`, a header row, RFC 4180 quoting); each a read that writes nothing, cookie or bearer, each GET named in `cookieRoute`'s read list; no `project` is `400 ProjectRequired`, an unregistered one `404 UnknownProject`, a bad range or band `400 SchemaError` naming the field, an absent store `503 AnalyticsNotBuilt {reason: "the store has not been built yet"}` (the page says so in words); every dollar field is documented as list price; `api.test.ts` pins each route's shape and refusal from a fixture store; the contract names the routes (0.1.36, additive) and the README the shapes in full.

**Given** the comparison method (research §7: the two sides by route or by date, per band, medians and ranges, three a side to speak, a fifth and non-overlapping interquartile ranges to say faster or slower; addendum §4 choice 6)
**When** `GET /analytics/compare?project=&change=<id>&metric=<cost_total_usd|cycle_s|hands_on_s|dev_s|review_passes|host_leg_s>&band=<S|M|L|all>&n=<window>` is called, or the route form `&stage=dev&route=<model/effort>&against=<model/effort>`
**Then** it answers `{ok, change, metric, band, before: {n, median, q1, q3, min, max, values: [{story, value, at}]}, after: {…}, verdict: faster | slower | cheaper | dearer | no-clear-change | not-enough-stories, verdict_text, caveats: [..]}`: the sides are the landed stories whose relevant stage row ran on the old and the new route (a change by date falls back to the date cut and says so in a caveat), the window `n` the newest `n` on each side (default 10), the band `all` answered only when both sides have the same band mix (else a caveat and per-band rows); `verdict_text` reads, for example, "Faster: the median dev time fell from 52 to 41 minutes over 6 and 5 M stories; the ranges do not overlap" or "Not enough stories yet: 2 of 3 on the new route"; `caveats[]` names the band mix, the review passes per side, the cache share where known, any other change between the two sides' dates, and regression to the mean when the change came within two stories of a guard trip or a red leg; the constants (three a side, a fifth) are two named exports in `analytics.ts`; `analytics.test.ts` pins faster, slower, no clear change, not enough stories, the band-mix caveat, the date fallback and the route form from fixture rows.

**Given** the owner's own marks (addendum §4 choice 7)
**When** `POST /analytics/notes {project, at, text}` is called (cookie + CSRF, or bearer; `text` at most 2 KiB, `at` an ISO time not in the future, else `400 SchemaError`)
**Then** the supervisor records an `analytics-note` service event, the next build adds the `changes` row (`kind: note`, `by: owner`), and the answer is the row; `POST /analytics/rebuild {project?, backfill?}` runs the verb once (refused `409 AnalyticsBuildRunning` while one runs) and answers the `meta` row; `api.test.ts` covers both and the refusal; the contract names them (0.1.36, additive).
