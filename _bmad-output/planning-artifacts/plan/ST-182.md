As the owner,
I want the inbox, the chat, a check-in, the status and a story to be usable on my phone from a notifier link, and the epic's records to be complete,
So that I can answer the studio from wherever I am, and the next epic starts from a true account (addendum §4 choice 5; D5).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Given** the text screens (`/app/inbox`, `/app/inbox/<KEY>`, `/app/chat`, `/app/checkin/<id>/<n>`, `/app/status`, `/app/story/<id>`, `/app/run/<id>`)
**When** opened at a phone width (375 px and 390 px, portrait) after Story 39.8
**Then** each lays out in one column with touch targets of at least 44 px, no horizontal scroll, the nav as a bottom bar, the answer controls reachable without zoom, and the office and the kanban show a notice "Best on a desktop or iPad; the office and the board are not laid out for a phone" with a link to the inbox; a vitest over the layout model and a documented manual check on a phone through the tailnet (recorded in the story spec's Consult Log) prove it; the notifier links open on the phone through the sign-in.

**Given** the epic's records
**When** this story lands
**Then** `apps/studio-web/README.md` carries one section per story of this epic, the design spec README's §6.3 points to the addendum, the addendum's §5 questions read answered or carried with the owner's words and dates, `kb/studio-front-end-epic-39.md` records the routes, the item kinds, the check-in model, the session hooks and the settings write path with file:line citations, every `by Story 39.x` ledger status carries its PR number (38.7's rule), the driver contract reads 0.1.35 with one paragraph per addition in story order, the measurements ledger carries the eight stories' engine costs, and the handoff names what stays the owner's (TKSTUDIO-233 to 236 and anything the epic adds); nothing else is decided here.
