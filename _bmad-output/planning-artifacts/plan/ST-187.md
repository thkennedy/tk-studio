As the owner,
I want to see each metric per story over time, and each stage over time as its own small chart, with every change I made drawn on the timeline where it happened,
So that a trend, a stall or a jump is visible, and I can see which change sits beside it (addendum §3.2 F-I; §3.6).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Given** `GET /analytics/stories`, `GET /analytics/stages` and `GET /analytics/changes` (Story 40.2) and the chart kit (Story 40.3)
**When** `/app/analytics/stories` is opened
**Then** it draws one dot per story over the time it landed, the y metric chosen from a select (cost at list price, cycle time, hands-on time, dev minutes, review passes, host-leg minutes), one hue, a facet row per band so sizes never share an axis, the changes as vertical hairlines in the de-emphasis gray with their label in text ink ("dev leg → claude-opus-5-5 / high, 2026-11-12"), hover and focus giving the story, the value, its band and the route its stage ran on, a click opening `/app/story/<id>`, and the table twin below (sortable, the CSV link to `GET /analytics/export.csv?table=stories`); the filter row of the overview scopes it; `src/analytics/stories.test.ts` pins the facets, the marks' positions and the labels from fixtures.

**Given** the same routes
**When** `/app/analytics/stages` is opened
**Then** it draws small multiples, one per stage (plan, dev, review, gate, host leg, close-out, queue wait), each with its own axis (never two y-axes on one plot), the per-story points with the rolling median of the last five as the line, the change marks on every chart, and, once a side has twelve points, an optional process-behaviour view per chart (the median and the natural limits from the moving range, labelled "trial limits" below twelve points and recomputed per point; a run of several consecutive points on one side of the old median said in words: "the last 6 stories sit above the median of the 12 before"); the pending state reads "Loading the stages…"; `src/analytics/stages.test.ts` pins the rolling median, the limits and the run text from fixtures.

**Given** `POST /analytics/notes` (Story 40.2)
**When** the owner presses "Add a note" on either view
**Then** a small form takes the time (default now) and the text, posts it, and the mark appears on the charts after the next build with "Note (owner): …" as its label, the hint reading "Marks appear after the next analytics build; press Rebuild to see it now"; `src/analytics/notes.test.ts` pins the form and the mark; `apps/studio-web/README.md` records the story.
