As the owner,
I want an analytics page that opens on the last 30 days in five tiles and a per-stage table, lists the recent stories, and tells me on any story's page how it compares with its band,
So that "how long" and "how much" are one glance away and every number says what it means (addendum §3.2 F-G, F-H; §3.6).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Given** the web's stack (`apps/studio-web/package.json`: Vite, React 19, TypeScript, one PixiJS canvas for the office only), the router of Story 39.1 and the dataviz method (addendum §3.1 principle 7, §3.7; research §6)
**When** Story 40.3 lands
**Then** `apps/studio-web/src/charts/` exists as the chart kit over `d3-scale`, `d3-shape` and `d3-array` (the only new dependencies, pinned in `package.json`, `bun install --frozen-lockfile` green): `StatTile` (label, value, signed delta against the named previous range, a 12-point sparkline), `Dots` (per-item points over time), `LineWithMedian`, `SmallMultiples`, `Dumbbell`, `ThinBar`, `AnnotationRule`, `Tooltip` (hover and keyboard focus, a hit area of at least 24 px) and `TableTwin`; colours come from one `palette.ts` holding the skill's reference values for light and dark (slot 1 for magnitude, the ordinal ramp for the bands, the status set for green and red legs, text in text tokens), and `tools/validate_palette.js` (the skill's script, vendored) runs in `bun run test` over the categorical set in both modes and fails on a FAIL; `src/charts/*.test.ts` pin the scales, the sparkline's points, the delta's sign and the table twin's rows; nothing in the kit imports `pixi.js`.

**Given** `GET /analytics/summary` and `GET /analytics/stories` (Story 40.2)
**When** `/app/analytics` is opened
**Then** the filter row (project, range presets 7 / 30 / 90 days / all with a custom range behind them, band) sits above everything it scopes; the five tiles read "Stories landed", "Median cycle time", "Median cost per story (at list price; the Max plan pays)", "First-leg green", "Spend in range" with their deltas against the previous range; the per-stage table lists stage, n, median minutes, range, median dollars and median tokens with a thin bar per row; the recent stories list links to `/app/story/<id>`; a definitions drawer gives each metric its one line ("Cycle time: from the plan's first start to the merge"); the pending states read "Loading the analytics…", a `503 AnalyticsNotBuilt` reads "The analytics store has not been built yet; the supervisor builds it after the next landed run" with a Rebuild button for the owner (`POST /analytics/rebuild`), and an empty range reads "No landed stories in this range yet"; a refetch holds the previous render at reduced opacity; `src/analytics/model.test.ts` pins the tiles, the deltas, the stage rows, the empty and the not-built states; `apps/studio-web/README.md` records the story.

**Given** the story page of Story 39.4 (`apps/studio-web/src/story/*`) and `GET /analytics/stories/{id}`
**When** a story page is opened after this story
**Then** it carries the strip "This story against its band": its cost, cycle, hands-on time, review passes and host-leg time, each against the band's median and range in words ("14.35 USD: above the band's median of 11.20; the range over 9 M stories is 8.68 to 38.96"), and its stage rows as a thin segmented bar of the cost split (plan, dev, review, gate, host leg, close-out in the fixed categorical order, a legend of names, the figures in the table beside it); a story with no analytics row reads "Not in the analytics yet (the store is built after each landed run)"; `src/story/model.test.ts` pins the strip's words and the bar's parts.
