As the owner,
I want Fable 5.2 accepted by the routing, the job definitions and the meter, Epic 41's remaining stories and the nightly pass pinned to it, and every such pin shown in words where I change the route,
So that "use Fable 5.2 for it" is a line in a file I can read and correct, a mistyped model is refused before a run starts, and what the pass costs is priced from a sourced figure.

**When** Story 41.2 lands
**Then** `claude-fable-5-2` is in `MODELS` and in `MODEL_RANK` at rank 3 (beside `claude-fable-5-1`; the advisor rule that an advisor is at least its executor's rank reads it), `job.py` refuses a job whose `model` is not in the one list (`pipeline.MODELS`, imported, not copied) with a message that names the accepted ids, at `resolve` and at `submit`, so a mistyped pin never reaches a spawn; `prices.json` gains the row only from the owner's or the operator's sourced figures (input, output, the two cache writes, cache read), with the file's date and `source` updated; if no figure has been given when the story runs, no row is written, the meter reports that model's runs as unpriced in words (never 0 and never another model's rate), the story's PR body says so under a heading of its own, and Story 41.6's nights do not begin on an unpriced model (41.5's spend hold treats an unpriced night as over); the tests pin the accepted id, the rank, the refused typo and the unpriced wording.
