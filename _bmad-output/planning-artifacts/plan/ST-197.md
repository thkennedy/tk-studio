As the owner,
I want the epic's records complete and the pass's state written down, including what it may not touch and what stays mine to decide,
So that the next epic starts from a true account and the nightly pass is something I can read about, pause and correct without asking.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Given** the epic's records
**When** this story lands
**Then** `apps/supervisor/README.md` carries one section per supervisor story of this epic (timed jobs, the hygiene run, the holds and the report, with every event's and route's shape in full), `plugins/tk-studio/lib`'s module docs name `hygiene.py`'s verbs and `launch.py hygiene`, `kb/nightly-hygiene-epic-41.md` records the timer's rules (UTC, arm on first sight, one fire for missed instants, wait and skip), the pass's bounds with file:line, the ledger's fields and `next`'s order, the model pins (the job's fields, the two routing rules, where each is read) and the list of what the pass does not do (TypeScript scanning, anything outside the three roots, behaviour-moving changes, the engine and the base, ClaudeOS) with what would widen each, every `by Story 41.x` ledger status carries its PR number (38.7's rule), the driver contract reads 0.1.37 with one paragraph per addition in story order, the measurements ledger carries the seven stories' engine costs, the plan PR's questions read answered with the owner's words and dates or carried with the default that ran, and the handoff names what stays the owner's (the ceilings, the night's hour, whether the pass widens to TypeScript or to a second class a night, the `prices.json` row if it is still missing, the sweep pile, which is not this pass's); nothing else is decided here.

**Given** the analytics store built over the epic's own runs and its first nights
**When** this story lands
**Then** `kb/nightly-hygiene-epic-41.md` carries a section "What Epic 41 and its first nights cost, read from the store": the seven stories' cycle time, cost and first-leg green from `GET /analytics/stories?epic=41`, the two stories that ran on Opus 5.5 beside the five that ran on Fable 5.2 (or all seven, if the owner chose the chore), and the nights so far from `GET /analytics/summary`'s `hygiene`; no figure in the section is typed by hand, and a figure the store cannot give (an unpriced model) is said in words.
