## Asset source cards

### Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator (characters)
- Coverage: layered 64x64 character generator with body/hair/eyes/torso/legs/feet/hat/tools/weapons layers; male base has idle, walk, run, sit, emote, combat_idle, climb, jump, hurt plus combat sets; no typing or celebrate sheet; newer animations lack layer coverage per README [https://api.github.com/repos/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/contents/spritesheets/body/bodies/male] [https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator].
- Licence (quoted): art is "licensed under one or more of ... CC0 ... CC-BY-SA ... CC-BY ... OGA-BY ... GPL"; CC-BY-SA is "the most restrictive license for any art supplied by this generator" and "allows you to use the art in this generator in commercial games"; code is GPL-3.0 [README; GitHub API].
- Price: free.
- Attribution duty: "you must at least credit all the authors (except for CC0 licensed artwork)"; ship CREDITS.csv or a composed list, "accessible from within your game or app" [README].
- Public-repo posture: redistribution with credits is what the licences require, so generated PNGs plus the generator's per-sprite credits CSV can be committed; derivative edits of LPC art must stay CC-BY-SA/GPL ("Must distribute any derivative artwork or modifications under CC-BY-SA 4.0 or later") [README]. The README speaks of "derivative artwork", not of code; whether a web app that merely loads the sheets is an adaptation was not evidenced this run (see Leads).
- Verdict: viable and active; strongest free option for a cast with idle/walk/sit; typing and celebrate would be custom frames (themselves share-alike if derived from LPC bodies); 64x64 style must be reconciled with 16x16 office tiles.

## Benefits and drawbacks

### All-CC0
- Benefits: no credits duty, no share-alike, commit everything, forks and contributors unencumbered (Kenney "Creative Commons CC0" [kenney.nl]).
- Drawbacks: no CC0 pack found this run with 16x16 top-down office furniture plus animated office-worker characters; nearest CC0 pieces are generic (surt), wrong scale (Danielfib 8x8), or static/unverified for animation (Kenney Roguelike Characters). Idle/walk/typing/thinking/celebrating would be custom drawn or generated; cohesion becomes the studio's own job.

### LPC share-alike characters (plus any tile source)
- Benefits: free; generator active (push 2026-10-07, 1,834 stars); idle, walk, sit, emote exist; attribution is mechanised via CREDITS.csv; commercial use explicitly allowed; sheets can be committed with credits.
- Drawbacks: must ship credits visibly in the app; any recolour or new frame derived from LPC art is CC-BY-SA/GPL ("Must distribute any derivative artwork or modifications under ..."); README itself flags version ambiguity (3.0 vs 4.0 text) and incomplete layer coverage for newer animations; no typing/celebrate sheets; 64x64 characters against 16x16 office tiles is a style-scale mismatch to resolve; the reach of share-alike into the app's code was not evidenced either way (README speaks only of "derivative artwork").

### Paid assets kept out of git (LimeZu)
- Benefits: best office coverage at $2.50 + $1.50 minimums; commercial use and editing allowed; credit is a single link; 4.8/4.9 ratings on 110/609 reviews.
- Drawbacks: "CAN'T resell or distribute the asset to others" means the public repo cannot carry the PNGs; every contributor and CI job needs a purchased copy dropped into a gitignored folder (officerun precedent: "contains no assets as they are not redistributable"); the office pack's last dev log is 2021-09-12; characters require the second pack; walk/sit/type coverage must be verified; build-time download and private-submodule patterns were not evidenced this run and a private submodule would still be a distribution to anyone granted access.

### Generated art (PixelLab, Retro Diffusion)
- Benefits: PixelLab ToS gives the user copyright and the right to "distribute the outputs ... for any purpose", so results can be committed; MCP/API fit an agent-driven studio; Retro Diffusion animation clips from $0.07; both produce walk cycles and multi-direction sprites.
- Drawbacks: plan prices for PixelLab unverified on a primary page; no practitioner evidence this run on cross-asset consistency; copyright status of AI output unverified (affects exclusivity, not the right to commit); Scenario's free tier is evaluation-only and its paid plans are subscriptions.
