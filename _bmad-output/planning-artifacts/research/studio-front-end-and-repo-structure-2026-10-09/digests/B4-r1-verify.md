## Notes

- Claim B4.1: Active repo: verified at the primary source (GitHub API; same source family as the original, so not strictly independent) with one correction: only `LiberatedPixelCup/...` is active (pushed 2026-10-07); `sanderfrenken/...` is a fork last pushed 2025-01-03, so the "or sanderfrenken" alternative should be dropped. Licence mix: the LPC style guide (independent publisher) confirms the base art is dual CC BY-SA 3.0 / GPL v3+, consistent with CC-BY-SA being the practical choice; whether every sheet in the generator offers a CC-BY-SA option (vs GPL-only pieces) was not checked beyond the original's CREDITS.csv row. Commercial use and share-alike: verified against the CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code (no NC clause; 3(b) ShareAlike on Adapted Material). Credits: `CREDITS.csv` exists at the repo root (3.99 MB); the generator's export-credits feature itself was not exercised. Animations: verified from the `female` body directory: idle, walk, sit, emote present; no typing, no celebrate (also no "dance" or "cheer"). Sizes: the style guide gives a 32x32 tile grid with 16x16 sub-tiles and a 48x64 clothing envelope; the 64x64 frame cell was not stated outright on the page (consistent, not directly verified). "Common office tilesets are 16x16": LimeZu's office, exteriors and farm packs are titled [16x16] on the itch.io sale page and OGA lists a 16x16 school set, but an 8x8 office tileset also exists, so "common" rather than "universal" is the right wording.
- Claim B4.2: Modern Office price: disputed. The itch.io sale page shows $2.50 as a 50% sale price with a $5.00 regular price; the sale ends 2026-10-09 17:10 UTC. LimeZu runs near-continuous seasonal sales (spring/summer/winter sale pages exist for 2025 and 2026), so $2.50 is the usual observed price, but the ledger should say "list $5.00, routinely $2.50 on sale". Modern Interiors "$1.50 or more": consistent with search snippets describing a $1.50 minimum for the paid tier; no page was fetched for it. Licence terms (commercial use, editing, no redistribution, credit): verified by three independent developers' READMEs and itch.io forum quotes; all three keep the files out of git. Counter-example recorded: SkyOffice (MIT, public) ships a LimeZu PNG; treat as a practice that contradicts the licence, not as evidence the licence allows it. PixelLab ToS: verified via TOS Tracker: dated 2025-11-23, users own outputs and may "use, modify, and distribute the outputs ... for any purpose" except model training. "No CC0 16x16 top-down office tileset with animated characters exists": unverified; the OGA listing shows no such pack but does not display licences, and no other catalogue was reachable within budget.
- Claim B4.3: ShareAlike scope: verified with nuance against the CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code: ShareAlike binds only "Adapted Material You produce", and Adapted Material requires modification "in a manner requiring permission", so an edited sprite is Adapted Material and must be shared alike. The legal code itself never says a collection or larger work is exempt; that rule comes from CC's interpretation page (the original source) and, for the 3.0 licence LPC actually uses, from 3.0's explicit "Collection" definition, which was not fetched within budget. The word "arranged" in the definition is the hook a counter-party could use, so keep the original's advice to ship LPC sprites unmodified in a clearly separable asset directory. LimeZu terms: verified (rows 6 and 11). PixelLab MCP metering: verified with nuance via a third-party wrapper listing: usage bills against "subscription generations" (roughly 1 generation for Pixflux, about 20 for style-matched generation, more for characters and tilesets) with a separate "USD credits" balance, so "metered in generations" is right but there are two balances. The TOS Tracker copy confirms programmatic access only via api.pixellab.ai; the `/mcp` path itself was only seen in search snippets of third-party MCP directories (lobehub, mcpservers.org), not fetched.
- Not reached within budget: LPC 64x64 frame size from an independent page; CC BY-SA 3.0 legal code "Collection" clause; a fetched Modern Interiors price page; any CC0 catalogue with licence filters; a fetched page naming api.pixellab.ai/mcp.
