## Intent

After a resume unblocks a story its issue sits in `Block Resolved`, an In Progress-category status that `promote` never leaves for `in-progress` or `review` (the category already matches) and that the pull-back reads back as canonical `blocked`, so a later `done` holds an AD-5 conflict for a block already closed (DW-151; the retrospective's B1); the picker ties `In Progress` with `Blocked - Awaiting User` for canonical `blocked` and lets the site's order decide (B2); the `in-progress` phase records the journal key while `review`/`done` record the spec stem with `-spec` (A4); `plansync setup` posts copied transitions without their `screen` and `rules` (DW-160); and the launch-time duties of 36.6 filter on `isRunEpic`, so a game project's `run-vertical-slice` run reads nothing In Progress and arms no stop (DW-159). This story fixes the four edges and the predicate, carries the transitions' configuration, and gives `run-vertical-slice.json` its rhythm.

## Acceptance Criteria

AC1. **Given** an issue in `Block Resolved` after a successful unblock (canonical `blocked`, DW-151) and a promote into the In Progress category where two routes share the canonical's preferred name (B2) **When** the plan-status pass promotes `review` or `done`, and when the picker ranks the routes **Then** a promote out of a block status for a non-`blocked` canonical moves on (`Block Resolved` → `In Progress`/`Done` is never held as an AD-5 conflict once the block is resolved), the pull-back maps `Block Resolved` to canonical `in-progress` (not `blocked`), the picker breaks a tie by the exact name then by the lowest transition id (never the list order), and the `story_key` spelled by the `in-progress`, `review` and `done` phases is one spelling (the story's key, no `-spec` suffix; A4); `test_jirabackend` and `planstatus.test.ts` pin each

AC2. **Given** `plansync.py setup` copying a workflow's transitions (Story 36.6; DW-160) **When** it posts `POST /workflows/update` **Then** each copied transition carries its `screen` and `rules` (validators, conditions, post functions) as the v3 read returns them, a transition whose read has fields the update schema does not accept is reported by name and left unchanged, and the lib suite's fake pins the shape; the live call stays the operator's through the consult log (Story 36.6's CAP-16, after this story lands)

AC3. **Given** a `run-vertical-slice` job (a game project's story run) and any later job type that runs stories **When** the launched path runs its duties (Story 36.6: the `in-progress` phase, the `stop-armed` rhythm) **Then** the duties apply by the job's `target` (a story payload), not by `isRunEpic` (DW-159), `run-vertical-slice.json` carries `after_stories`, and the supervisor suites cover a vertical-slice run marked In Progress and armed; the contract names the rule (0.1.32, additive)

## Risks and open decisions

- **oversize**: ~22 files, ~450-550 lines (production 120-170: jirabackend 60-80, the supervisor ~30, JSON/schema/launch ~10, contract 25-35; tests 300-400). Halt (`halt` consult) if the Files list grows past the band — the dev session never splits the story. Split candidates if the clock threatens: (i) the Jira edges and the one spelling; (ii) `screen`/`rules` and the duties by `target`.
- Block If the v3 update refuses the legacy spellings of `screen`/`rules` in the fake's `allowed` set and no shape makes the fake accept them: the design note's `partial` with `refused_transitions` and nothing posted is the answer; consult (`bad_spec`) before inventing v3 spellings (DW-160's are unverified; the live call is the operator's).
- Block If a promote out of `Blocked - Awaiting User` is needed by a test the AC implies (its "a promote out of a block status" read literally): the design note keeps the block winning while it stands; consult (`bad_spec`) rather than move a standing block.
- Block If the explicit `stop` on the slice mint must differ by the engagement's autonomy (36.6's comment): the design note mints `after_stories: 1` for every slice; consult (`deviation-gt-0`) if an autonomy figure is asked for.
- Not a Block If: transition ids are strings; a non-numeric id ranks last (`inf`), never a refusal.
- Not a Block If: events already recorded with `-spec` stay; both readers keep their strip.
- Not a Block If: the live `plansync setup` is 37.7's consult ask, gated on this story being on `main`.
- `bun` is absent on the launch host: the supervisor suites run in the engine sandbox; record the exact commands as `unverifiable-here` if the sandbox cannot run them.
