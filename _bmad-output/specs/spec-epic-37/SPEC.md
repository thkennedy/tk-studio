# Epic 37: Loop Hygiene 4, the Ledger, the Host Leg and the Clock

## Why

A pain to solve. Epic 36 closed on 2026-10-31 `accepted-with-open-items` (`spec-epic-36/RETROSPECTIVE.md`, PR #322): seven stories in six runs, every PR merged on a green host leg, but two holes recurred inside the epic. Hole 107, the ledger id collision, hit twice (DW-133/134 → DW-146/147 at `90a2dfb`, DW-151 → DW-153 at `61c0955`) because both writers mint from the ledger's highest heading on whichever branch they are on; hole 110, a Windows-only difference the Linux gate cannot see, hit three times (#302 on time bounds, hole 108; #307 on a `path.join` into `toVmPath` and a rename over an empty folder; #313 on a POSIX-suffix assertion, after 36.4's encoding drive landed), every one fixed by hand on the branch. Run 1 parked on 36.3's dev clock with its stop discarded (holes 105, 106): a 6.3M-token attempt with no Stage Log row, and the cancelled run's `job-run` line with no worker cost. The sweep's cost and end rules (36.2, 36.3) are proven under fakes only, with B3 to B5 and DW-136 to DW-139, DW-144 open before a fourth sweep can run; the Jira status edges B1/B2 and the `story_key` spelling A4 hold a story's issue in a block status; four callers build the engine's stop two ways (A2, DW-162) and the cycle test is blind to dynamic imports (A1, DW-164); the Epic 36 records carry stale text and four operator proofs are owed (A5, C1). The retrospective's AI-10 orders the next plan: AI-1 and AI-2 land before any new surface. This is the fourth loop-hygiene pass (Epics 33, 35 and 36 were the first three), planned from the retrospective's action items AI-1 to AI-8 and the open deferred-work entries DW-133 to DW-164 that are the loop's own (the follow-up-review entries DW-145, 152, 156, 158, 163 and the owner's decisions, AI-9, stay Tim's), in that order of authority, then the handoff's day-8 updates and the contract moving from 0.1.31 to 0.1.32 (additive) at 37.1.
