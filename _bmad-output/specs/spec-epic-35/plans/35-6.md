## Intent

The engagement's closeout page carries each slice's contact sheet and verdicts but not its sampled frames or `report.json`, and every story of a run embeds the run's **last** leg's sheet (DW-77; DW-92's first edge). Story 35.6 gives each leg its own copy of the report and sampled frames beside the sheet it already copies, has `composeCloseout` pick each story's own leg, and carries `report` and `frames` through the closeout input to the page: the files are attached and a verdict table is embedded. It also closes the retro's five smaller B15 edges, each with a test (DW-92). Contract 0.1.30 gains an additive Story 35.6 paragraph. Repo: tk-studio (`apps/supervisor`, `plugins/tk-studio` lib and contracts).

## Settled shape

Bullet 1 below is appended to `SPEC.md` `## Design notes (settled shape, planner, 2026-10-27)` as the CAP-13 note; bullets 2 to 4 are its detail.

1. **CAP-13: the per-leg proof (optional entry).** On a slice leg whose sheet is made, the leg copies the store's `report.json` and the sheet's sampled frames into `<store>/legs/<stamp>/report.json` and `<store>/legs/<stamp>/frames/<name>`, where `<stamp>` is the same `<ms>-<pid>-<seq>` as its `<store>/sheets/<stamp>.png`. The `playable` member gains `report?: "<abs path>"` and `leg_frames?: ["<abs path>", …]` (the sampled set, in sheet order, at most 16). The copy is best-effort, like the sheet: a failed copy leaves both fields out and never sets a verdict. `frames` and `sampled` keep their 0.1.29 meaning.
2. **CAP-13: a story's own leg (named placeholder rule).**
   - Each story key's leg is the latest `host-verify` event in that story's window.
   - A story's window runs from just after the previous different story's `close-out` up to and including its own `close-out`.
   - The run's last story (by `close-out` order) also gets every `host-verify` after its `close-out`: the re-runs on the branch tip.
   - A `landedStory` key with no `close-out` takes the window after the run's last `close-out`.
   - A window with no `host-verify` reads `not run`. The 34.8 verdict mapping, the one-entry-per-story Map and the later-linked-run override stay.
3. **CAP-13: the closeout input's media and the page (optional entry).**
   - `slices[]` gains `report?: "<abs path>"` and `frames?: ["<abs path>", …]`, from the chosen leg's `playable.report` and `playable.leg_frames`. Both are paths under the run workspace.
   - `engage.py closeout` reads them like the sheet. Each file is written beside `closeout.md` and attached:
     - the report as `<key>-report.json`;
     - each frame as `<key>-frame-<name>`, where `<name>` is the frame's basename.
   - An unreadable file is a note and is dropped.
   - Under the story's bullet in `## Slice verdicts`, after the sheet image, the page has:
     - a two-column GFM table `| Report | Value |` with rows `verdict`, `failed` (`<invariant>: <detail>` or `—`), `ticks` (`<ticks_run>/<ticks>`), `seed`, `mode`, `moments` (`<name>@<tick>` joined by `, `, or `—`) and `log errors` (the count);
     - then one line `Frames: [<name>](<key>-frame-<name>), …` and `[report.json](<key>-report.json)`.
   - A report that is not a JSON object gives a note and no table.
   - A slice with neither field renders exactly as in 0.1.29.
4. **CAP-13: the engagement conformance drive (named placeholder rule).**
   - Add one `headless-clean` drive under `tk-studio-engage`: `contracts/conformance/e2e_closeout_media.py`. It is stdlib and in process, with no bun and no network.
   - In a temp served root it builds an `in-progress` engagement record and its scope, two slice stories with distinct legs' `report.json`, frames and sheets, and the closeout input.
   - It runs `engage.closeout` with a fake Confluence transport, using the same fake shape as `test_engage.py`'s `FakeEngagementConfluence`, copied into the drive and not imported from tests.
   - It asserts that `closeout.md` carries each story's own sheet, its frame links and the verdict table, and that the attachments hold `<key>-report.json` and the frames.
   - It prints `{ok, steps[]}`.
   - The supervisor side (Settled shape 2) is covered by `delivery.test.ts` in the supervisor suite.
