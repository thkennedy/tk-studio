# Epic 35: Loop Hygiene 2, the Slice Path Hardened

## Why

A pain to solve. Epic 34 closed on 2026-10-27 `accepted-with-open-items` (`spec-epic-34/RETROSPECTIVE.md`): every capability of the vertical-slice pack is built and pinned, and what it left open is the loop's own. 33.8's verify verb re-runs the host leg in a throwaway `.verify` worktree that commits nothing and leaves the PR body's `## Host verify` section as the first leg wrote it, so 4 of Epic 34's 7 story PRs read Red at merge while the verb had gone green. The launch bootstrap plans the first story only, and a resumed run that crosses a story boundary records the last story only (the 34.5 episode: no gate file, no reconcile, no cost line for 34-5). The engine's own `bun test` in the sandbox reports 15 Story 32.8 delivery tests red at baseline that the host passes; two of Story 34.8's three eval cases cannot run on the host. The per-run engine MCP (34.5) takes one fixed port per box and lets go of its children before recording the start. The deferred-work sweep runs only inside a bmad-loop sweep session (`BMAD_LOOP_MODE=1`, `[sweep] auto = "never"`) and no job starts one, so DW-46 to DW-65 and the retrospective's deferred findings have no path to a PR. This is the second loop-hygiene pass (Epic 33 was the first), planned from the retrospective's action items (AI-2, AI-3, AI-5, AI-9; findings B1 to B16, C2, C4, C5), the observation ledger's holes 78, 81, 84, 85, 88 and 89 (rows of the runbook's fixes table) and DW-76 to DW-94, in that order of authority, then the handoff's day-7 addenda and the contract at 0.1.29. What exists today and is extended, never replaced: the host leg's `verify.host`, engine gate, assets step, `playable.py` and judge on a run's close-out (Stories 27.4, 33.4, 34.1, 34.3, 34.6 to 34.8).

## Constraints

- Where things run (kept from Epic 34): every `claude` session (an engine) runs only in the project's Docker Sandbox, never with bypass on the host, and the engine never runs on the host; the host leg runs deterministic scripts and the engine binaries, never a model; secrets reach a worker by name only.
- Surface rules: every surface change is additive on the contract at 0.1.30 and up, ends in a status block (AD-11), is covered by conformance (AD-19) and lands its cost in the ledger.
- Cadence: a fix to the loop lands through the loop, one story per run with a graceful stop after each and the host leg green before the PR; an operator PR that touches lib, its tests or the contract runs the whole host gate before merge (hole 86); seven stories in dependency order, the re-run (35.1) first because every later story's fix-forward relies on it, the sweep (35.7) last over whatever the ledger still holds.
- Decisions taken, not reopened: the retrospective's dispositions stand; DW-76 (B9) is decided as "the re-run is a whole leg"; AI-4's visual-gate floor stays 0.25 (DW-93).
- Tier asks: where a story's proof needs the host or the sandbox tier (a flagged run on TUA, an eval run, a sweep), the engine asks through the spec's consult log and the operator session does that step and answers with the evidence; a sweep executes a bundle only as a supervisor run with a worktree, a host leg and a PR, never a direct commit.
- Repo: tk-studio only (`apps/supervisor` hostleg, worktree, enginemcp, hostverify, delivery, env; `plugins/tk-studio/lib` `playable.py`, `assets.py`, `playable_judge.py`, `recommend.py`, `engage.py`, `launch.py`; the contracts and conformance drives; `plugins/tk-studio/jobs`; the skills `tk-studio-playable-judge` and `tk-studio-launch`; `kb/` and the runbook); a TUA change lands only through a TUA run PR or the operator's step on the box.

## Non-goals

- Reopening an Epic 34 disposition or the 0.25 visual-gate floor.
- A sweep that commits to main directly or runs outside the supervisor.
- The engine on the host; a model on the host leg.
- A non-additive contract change; a second bump beyond 0.1.30 unless a story cannot extend it additively.
- Breaking a story into sub-tasks.
- The TUA acceptance itself and the design spec's feature-ledger Phase 3 row (the operator's, AI-1).
- Fixing DW-46 to DW-65 or DW-85 to DW-87 inside a story: they go through 35.7's sweep.
- Exposing the engine MCP's scene-editing tools (verify-only stays).

## Success signal

- A fixed-forward run's PR reads the truth at merge: the `## Host verify` section rewritten and the PR ready on a green re-run, a slice re-run's art and proof pushed. A relaunched epic reaches every dev session with a plan, and a two-story resume leaves two gate files, two reconciles and two cost lines (or is refused). A slice run's red names the game or the asset, and an infrastructure failure reads `inconclusive`. Two flagged runs start on one box and the sandbox reaches the forwarder on TUA. The sandbox's delivery suite is green at baseline and the two eval cases score on the sandbox tier; an unplaced engine job is refused `placement:TierRequired`. The closeout page carries frames and report per slice. One `run-sweep` on the sandbox tier ends in merged PRs with ledger entries resolved. Conformance green at 0.1.30 or later; every story's cost in the ledger.

## Assumptions

- One contract bump, 0.1.30, opens at 35.1 and every later story extends its entry additively (35.2 the per-story records rule, 35.3 `inconclusive`, 35.4 the ports and the advisory, 35.5 `requires.tier`, 35.6 the closeout media shape, 35.7 the job type), as 0.1.29 did in Epic 34.
