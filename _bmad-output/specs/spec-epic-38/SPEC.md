This is the fifth loop-hygiene pass (Epics 33, 35, 36 and 37 were the first four), planned from the retrospective's action items AI-1 to AI-7 and the loop's own open deferred-work entries DW-165 to DW-177, DW-180, DW-182 and DW-183, in that order of authority; the follow-up-review entries the cap spent (DW-145, 152, 156, 158, 163, 181), the owner's designs (DW-178 the check-in model, DW-179 the nightly hygiene pass: new surface, Epic 39 candidates on Tim's word), TKSTUDIO-220 to 224 and the sweep-domain entries DW-26 to DW-129 stay Tim's or the fourth sweep's.
