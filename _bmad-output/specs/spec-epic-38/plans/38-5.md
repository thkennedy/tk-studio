## Intent

Story 37.3 made the engine's clock a pause with a bounded exit, but its first cut left four holes (the Epic 37 retrospective's B5 to B8) and three ledger entries (DW-166, DW-176, DW-177): every non-sweep pause runs a full host leg inline in the engines loop before the `awaiting-ruling` notice goes out, a pause leg and the later boundary end run two legs on the same commits, a checkout with no policy gets a stub that hides the sweep's gap check and a non-bare `[scm]` form fails the edit, `own_block` resubmissions are unbounded, an engine-clock pause tags the owner, the late close-out paths still mark a draft ready with no leg, and a split story's `<key>-s2` spec makes the first half's spec lookup ambiguous. This story finishes the path (AI-5): notice and block first, the leg in its own pass, one leg per code head, a refusal instead of a stub and a TOML-aware edit, `stop.max_own_blocks`, `track.py block --kind engine`, the three late paths' draft and records, and one split-spec resolution rule in three places, the contract's 0.1.34 entry extended (additive).
