## Intent

Epics 34 and 35 left the supervisor with an eleven-module import cycle and three dynamic-import dodges, the lib with `assets ⇄ playable`, six copies of the status-block scaffolding and three of the user-env read, `assets.py` at 2,439 lines and `engage.py scope` grown past its Epic 34 size (DW-85, DW-86, DW-87), and the docs a few rows short (AI-7; the EP-35 retro hygiene commit `fba5ef9` already landed the root README rows). This story cuts the named cycles to one-way seams and pins zero cycles among the named modules with a test in the host gate, removes the dodges, folds the named duplication into shared helpers, splits `assets.py` along its leaves as far as one story carries (the remainder stated on the entry), shrinks `scope`, and lands the residual docs — behaviour pinned by the existing suites, no surface change. It is last because it refactors the modules every earlier story edits.
