## Intent
The meter's per-story observation gains structured, additive fields, so the studio's own history can be read as numbers instead of a free-text line. `engage.py quote` reads that history for the project and resamples per-story tokens over the scope's proposed story count. The result is a seeded 50/85/95 band in tokens, shown as plan-window share with a list-price dollar shadow, plus a hard cap. Under eight landed stories the band says "thin history" and is widened by the design spec's §10 analogy. The quote lands in the record and the planner proposes `go`. The owner's `go` moves `scoping → quoted` through 32.1's existing `quote-present` row (F2 step 4; D4; B5 item 4).

## Acceptance Criteria
- AC1: **Given** the meter's per-story records (today a free-text `observation`; this story makes the meter's payload structured and additive: `kind: meter`, `story_key`, `epic`, `usd`, `tokens {input, output, cache_read, cache_write}` per leg and the route, written to `transcripts/<key>/observation.json` and harvested to the host ledger as before) and the scope's proposed stories **When** `engage.py quote` runs (the planner's turn after the scope, or by hand) **Then** it computes, for this project and story kind, a seeded bootstrap (10,000 resamples of per-story tokens over the proposed story count) 50/85/95 band in tokens, shown as plan-window share (the plan's weekly window from `~/.tk-studio/config.yaml`'s `plan.window_tokens`, else "share unknown") with the list-price dollar shadow (`prices.json`), the cap (`cap = p95 + one story at p85`) and what happens at the cap (the run pauses `partial`, the engagement's issue blocks with the figures, the owner decides), the route and the autonomy levels on offer (32.6), and whether scoping was billable (the planning spend so far); a history under eight landed stories says "thin history" and widens the band from the design spec §10's sizing by analogy, never a bare number; `quote {band50, band85, band95, cap, basis, computed_at}` lands in the record, the state moves `scoping → quoted`, and `.tk-studio/budget-bands.yaml` gains `epics.<proposed EP>.usd_estimate` from the 85th so the board draws the band against spend once the epics exist
- AC2: **Given** the quote **When** it is tested **Then** the lib suite covers the resampling (seeded, exact), the thin-history rule, the share, the cap and the voiding on `revise` with fixtures; `GET /engagements/{id}` carries the quote and `GET /board`'s `band_usd` follows it; `kb/quote-band.md` records the method and its first figures

## Settled shape
1. **CAP-10, the meter's structured payload** (`optional entry`). Optional `observation` payload fields, added to `taxonomy.v1.json`:
   - `kind` (string; `"meter"`)
   - `story_key` (string)
   - `epic` (number, the story key's leading integer)
   - `usd` (number, `usd_total`)
   - `legs` (object: `<leg>: {usd, usage: {input, output, cache_read, cache_write}}`)
   - `route` (object, leg → model)
   - `prices_date` (string)

   Why `usage` and not the AC's `tokens`: the ledger sanitizer redacts any key matching `token` wholesale (`classify.py` `CREDENTIAL_KEY_RE`), a credential rule this story must not weaken. The AC's `tokens {input, output, cache_read, cache_write}` therefore lands as `legs.<leg>.usage {…}` with the same four fields. Also:
   - The description string is unchanged.
   - `observation.json` equals the ledger line, as today.
   - `harvest` re-emits the same structured fields.
   - A record without `kind: meter` is history the quote does not read.

   Per Story 32.5 AC1, `ledger.py` validate/sanitize, `classify.py:21-23`. SPEC.md bullet: not appended (a gate session may not edit SPEC.md); this section is the record.
2. **CAP-10, the quote** (`named placeholder rule`). `engage.py quote --directory <project_root> --engagement ENG-NNN [--seed N] [--dry-run]`.
   - **Refusals:** a non-JSON record is `EngagementRecordUnreadable`. A terminal state is `EngagementClosed`. Not `scoping` is `NotScoping`. A missing `scope.md` or zero proposed stories is `NoScope`. An unreadable `prices.json`, or an unpriced route model, is `PricesUnreadable`. An `OSError` is `QuoteUnwritable`.
   - **History:** per-story total tokens `t = Σ legs (input + output + cache_read + cache_write)` and per-story `usd`, from the project's structured meter records (Code Map, deduped by `story_key`).
   - **Resampling:** `random.Random(seed)`. The default seed is `int.from_bytes(sha256(f"{engagement}:{n}").digest()[:8], "big")`, recorded in `basis.seed`. 10,000 resamples. Each resample draws `n` story indices with replacement (`n` = the proposed story count) and sums `t` and `usd` over the **same** indices.
   - **Percentiles:** nearest-rank on the sorted sums. `band50`, `band85` and `band95` are each `{tokens, usd, share}`.
   - **Share:** `share = tokens / plan.window_tokens` (4 decimals), or the string `"share unknown"`.
   - **Cap:** `cap = band95 + p85 of a one-story resample` (in tokens and usd, same seed stream). `quote.cap` is a number in USD (what `raise-cap` adds to), and `basis.cap_tokens` carries the tokens.
   - **`basis`:** `{method: "bootstrap", resamples: 10000, seed, stories: n, epics: [{k, title, stories, ep_id}], history: {n, unstructured, project}, kind: "story", thin: bool, at_cap: "the run pauses partial, the engagement's issue blocks with the figures, the owner decides", route, autonomy: ["ask", "steady", "full"], planning_usd: <spend.planning_usd>, scoping_billable: planning_usd > 0, prices_date, window_tokens | null, cap_tokens}`. `computed_at` is ISO UTC.
   - **Story kind:** `"story"` (project-wide). The meter carries no story kind today. The basis names it, so a later kind split is additive.
   - **Record:** merge only `quote` and `updated` into a fresh read. The state does not move here (Settled shape 3).

   Per Story 32.5 AC1, `engagement.ts` `raise-cap` (:271). SPEC.md bullet: not appended (as 1).
3. **CAP-10, how `scoping → quoted` moves** (`named placeholder rule`, SPEC Open Question 1 read as the 32.1 plan reads it).
   - The quote is a file write by the planner. The state moves by the owner's `go` through 32.1's existing row `scoping + go → quoted [quote-present]` (`engagement.ts:209`).
   - The planner's reply carries a `proposals[]` entry `{verb: "go", payload: {}, label: "Accept the quote"}`, which `SKILL.md` instructs.
   - Why: the design spec §7.1 says only a typed input moves the state; F2 step 5 says leaving planning is an explicit exit; and the SPEC Constraints name 32.3's `question` as the one planner exception.
   - No `TRANSITIONS` row is added.

   SPEC.md bullet: not appended (as 1).
4. **CAP-11, `budget-bands.yaml` `epics`** (`named placeholder rule`).
   - **Keys:** each proposed epic `k` gets the id it is expected to receive at approve, `EP-<next.epic + k − 1>` from `.tk-studio/plan-counter.yaml` (three digits, as `plansync` mints). That id is recorded in `basis.epics[].ep_id`, so 32.6 can re-key when the counter moved.
   - **Value:** `usd_estimate` is the p85 `usd` of a bootstrap over that epic's own story count (same seed stream), rounded to cents.
   - **Write:** atomic. Every byte before the top-level `epics:` key is kept, and only the `epics:` block is replaced or appended (the comments and `bands:` stay). Entries of other epics are kept, and an entry this quote owns is overwritten.
   - The tracked file still ships no `epics` entry (`apps/supervisor/README.md:189-197` stays true). The write happens in the served root at quote time.
   - A `revise` voids `quote` (32.1) and leaves the entries until the next quote overwrites them.

   Per Story 32.5 AC1, `board.ts` `readBands` (:241-253). SPEC.md bullet: not appended (as 1).
5. **CAP-10, thin history** (`named placeholder rule`). With fewer than 8 structured records for the project, `basis.thin = true` and the result's notes say `thin history: <n> landed stories`.
   - **Samples:** the resampled set is the project's records plus the analogy prior. The prior is the twelve per-story all-in dollars of the Opus 5.5 trial (`kb/trial-record-opus-5-5.md` per-story table: Epics 20–22, the per-story breakdown behind design spec §10's analogy). It is held in `quote.py` as a dated, sourced table.
   - **Tokens for a prior sample:** its dollars ÷ the history's mean usd-per-token when n ≥ 1. With n = 0, the band's `tokens` and `share` are `null` and the notes say `tokens unknown: thin history (no metered story)`. Dollars always carry, so the band is never a bare number.
   - **Widening:** `band85`, `band95` and the cap are each multiplied by 1.5. The basis names `widen: 1.5` and `prior: "kb/trial-record-opus-5-5.md"`.

   Per Story 32.5 AC1 and design spec §10 (:268). SPEC.md bullet: not appended (as 1).

## Risks and open decisions
- **Oversize:** 16 files across the meter, the ledger taxonomy, a new quote core, the engage verb, the contract and two supervisor test files, past band L. Not split here (task-planning §3). HALT `blocked` if the Files list must grow beyond this plan's list.
- **Block If:** the quote can only move `scoping → quoted` without the owner's `go`, through a new `TRANSITIONS` row or a supervisor apply pass. HALT. That would be a second planner exception (SPEC Open Question 1), which is the operator's ruling.
- **Block If:** the structured meter fields can only land by weakening `ledger.sanitize` or `classify.CREDENTIAL_KEY_RE`. HALT. Settled shape 1 avoids it with `usage`.
- **Block If:** a change to `meter.py` alters an existing observation's description string or breaks `test_meter.py`'s pinned shape. HALT naming the test.
- Not Block Ifs:
  - **No history today.** No structured meter record exists until stories land after this one, so the first quotes are thin history. With no metered story at all they are dollars-only. `kb/quote-band.md` records whatever figures exist.
  - **The `EP-NNN` prediction can go stale.** If another engagement is approved first, the plan counter moves and the predicted keys go stale. `basis.epics[].ep_id` lets 32.6 re-key at approve.
  - **The planner's next turn may not offer `go`.** The `go` proposal is SKILL guidance; the owner can always send `go` from the API.
  - **Every machine's ledger file is read.** The quote reads all files under the store's `measurements/` (other machines' harvested records included), filtered by project.
  - **No shared record lock** (the 32-2 consult): `quote` keeps the fresh-read, merge-only rule.
  - The 3 released-roster guards (Verify 1).
