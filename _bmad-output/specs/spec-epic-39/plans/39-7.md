## Intent

Today the owner's inbox is only the Jira blocks the sync-back writes to `inbox.json`. Under the `ask` autonomy he can only continue from a Jira comment. That premise was rejected by his ruling on DW-48 (TKSTUDIO-215, recorded as DW-178).

This story makes `GET /inbox` one list holding five kinds of item:
- `decision`: Jira blocks;
- `question`: engagement questions;
- `checkin`: reached check-ins;
- `notice`: notifier lines, from a new `notices.jsonl` the senders append to;
- `message`: studio-chat lines.

Each item carries a `kind` and the `route` that answers it, and the list is sorted "needs you" first.

It also replaces the `ask` pause with check-ins agreed at approval (`checkins[]` on the record). Each kind holds the engagement's queueing in a different way:
- a reached check-in holds queueing after the running story;
- continue and stop are answered at `POST /engagements/{id}/checkin/{n}` from a `/app/checkin/<id>/<n>` page;
- stop closes the engagement and the studio visibly;
- a Jira `continue` stays as the logged fallback.

The web gains the inbox's answer controls in place and the check-in page. DW-178 and DW-48 close.

## Settled shape

1. **The check-in entry (CAP-22, SPEC design note).** `Engagement.checkins?: Checkin[]` (absent on records approved before 39.7, read as `[]`). Each entry:
   ```
   {n: int ≥ 1 (its position + 1), kind: "milestone" | "budget" | "proof", when, what_to_show: ("pr" | "board" | "proof" | "spend")[],
    added?: "ask" | "owner", reached_at?: ISO, shown?: {story_key?, run_id?, pr?, board?, proof?, spend?}, decision?: {decision: "continue" | "stop", note: string | null, at: ISO, via: "cookie" | "bearer" | "jira"}}
   ```
   `when` by kind:
   - milestone: `"first-story"`, `"end"`, a story key `^\d+-\d+$`, or `"now"` (the route's, never in `approve`);
   - budget: a USD number > 0;
   - proof: `"playable"`.

   The supervisor writes `added`, `reached_at`, `shown` and `decision`; the approval writes the rest. `what_to_show` defaults per kind: milestone `[pr, board, spend]`, budget `[spend, board]`, proof `[proof, board, spend]`.

2. **The reach conditions (CAP-22).** All are scoped to the newest approve cycle (the links and events at or after `approveLine(record).at`; a re-approval writes fresh `checkins`).
   - `first-story`: any linked run of the cycle has `landedStory`.
   - A key K: a landed story whose key is K or starts with `K-`.
   - `end`: an ok `engagement-closeout` event of the cycle.
   - budget X: `spend.runs_usd >= X`.
   - proof `playable`: a linked run's `paused`, `ended` or `closed_out` event carrying `detail.closeout.host_commit`.

   `shown` renders only the listed keys:
   - `pr`: the landed story's PR (`runPr(queue.events(run))`), else null;
   - `board`: `/app/board/<project>`;
   - `proof`: `.tk-studio/proof/<run_id>/` (POSIX, root-relative);
   - `spend`: `{runs_usd, band_usd: quote.band85.usd | null, cap: quote.cap | null}`.

3. **The defaults: Q4 (CAP-22, SPEC design note settling the Open Question).** `defaultCheckins(quote)`:
   ```
   [{n:1, milestone, "first-story", [pr, board, spend]},
    {n:2, budget, round2(quote.band85.usd / 2) (else round2(quote.cap / 2); neither: this entry left out), [spend, board]},
    {n:3 (or 2), milestone, "end", [pr, board, spend]}]
   ```
   - `approve {autonomy, base_ref?, checkins?}`: `checkins` is optional. Absent, `record-approval` records the defaults. `[]` records none.
   - The Approve card shows the same proposal (`proposeCheckins`, mirrored in `src/chat/model.ts`) and edits it.
   - The question stays open for the owner (addendum §5 Q4). The story's default is these three (§3.6).
   - Records approved before 39.7 take no defaults.
   - Validation is `400 SchemaError` with a detail naming the entry:
     - at most 12 entries;
     - each an object with only `n`, `kind`, `when` and `what_to_show`;
     - `n`, when given, equals the position + 1;
     - `when` per shape 1, `now` refused;
     - `what_to_show` a non-empty subset.

4. **The hold (CAP-22).** Run-pass step 3, which replaces the `ask` branch, for every reached entry:
   - (a) `reached_at` and `shown` are written through `writeFresh` (once).
   - (b) While the record is `in-progress` and the entry is not `end`:
     - every live linked run of the cycle is stopped gracefully with reason `checkin` and `story_key` when known (`stopRun`: the engine finishes its running story);
     - a still-queued one is cancelled (`cancelQueued`, reason `checkin`);
     - then `run-paused {checkin: n, run_id?, story_key?}` is applied once.
   - (c) Told once: no `engagement-checkin {action: "reached", n}` event of the cycle yet. Then:
     - the notifier line, `EndNotice {state: "checkin", reason: "engagement <id> check-in <n> of <N>: <kind in words> — continue or stop"}` with 39.1's route `/app/checkin/<id>/<n>`;
     - the notice line;
     - `engagement-checkin {engagement, project, n, of, kind, when, action: "reached", run_id: string|null, story_key: string|null, runs_usd, route, notify}`.
   - (d) Jira-bound: the existing step 5 blocks the issue `--kind ask` (`track.py` unchanged), owed since that `run-paused` line. The reason is `check-in <n> of <N>: <kind in words>\n<evaluate lines>\ncontinue or stop at <absolute route>; or reply continue here (the fallback)`.
   - Step 7 resubmits after a `checkin` stop (or a legacy `ask` stop) only once a `run-resumed` line follows it. The merge gate applies when any ok stop of the run is `steady` **or** `record.autonomy === "steady"`.

5. **The `ask` autonomy (CAP-22).** `ask` means check-ins at every story. On an `ask` record, step 3 first appends, for each landed story of the cycle that no reached entry names, `{n: max+1, kind: "milestone", when: <story key>, what_to_show: [pr, board, spend], added: "ask"}`, and reaches it in the same pass. On an `ask` record with no `checkins` field (approved before 39.7), the field is created that way.
   - The old pause path is retired:
     - step 3's `ask` stop, its bare `run-paused` and the P7 re-apply;
     - step 5's "waits for the owner's continue" text;
     - step 6's `ask`-only condition.
   - `steady` and `full` keep their behaviour and reach their own check-ins.
   - A legacy `ask` pause in flight (a `run-paused` line with no `checkin`) still resumes through the Jira fallback (shape 7).

6. **The terminal state and the table rows (CAP-22, SPEC design note).**
   - `ENGAGEMENT_STATES` gains `closed`, last, and `TERMINAL_STATES` gains it too, giving `delivered, failed, cancelled, closed`. The `ENGAGEMENT_EVENTS` list gains `checkin-stop`.
   - The new rows, source `supervisor`, each with the detail `{checkin: n, via, note?}`:
     - `approved | in-progress | paused | partial` event `checkin-stop` → `closed`.
   - `engage.py TERMINAL_STATES` gains `closed`.
   - The web's mirror gains `closed` (`ACTIONS.closed = []`, a side exit), with the state text `closed: stopped at check-in <n> by the owner`.

7. **The route (CAP-22, SPEC design note).** `POST /engagements/{id}/checkin/{n}[?project=]`, cookie + CSRF or bearer.
   - `{n}`: `^[1-9]\d*$`, or `now` (shape 8).
   - Body: `{decision: "continue" | "stop", note?: string}`, `note` non-empty and at most 32 KiB UTF-8. No other field is accepted.
   - Answer `200 {ok: true, engagement: <record>, checkin: <entry>, from, to}`.

   Refusals, in order:

   | Status | Error | When |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | 400 | `MalformedJson` | the body is not JSON |
   | 400 | `SchemaError {detail}` | the body breaks the shape |
   | 400 | `BadEngagement` | the id is not an engagement id |
   | 400 | `BadCheckin {engagement, checkin}` | `{n}` is neither shape |
   | 403 | `CsrfRefused` | a cookie session without the token |
   | 404 | `UnknownProject` | the project does not exist |
   | 404 | `UnknownEngagement` | the engagement does not exist |
   | 409 | `AmbiguousEngagement` | more than one holder |
   | 500 | `EngagementRecordInvalid` | the record is unreadable |
   | 404 | `UnknownCheckin {engagement, n, of}` | no such entry |
   | 409 | `EngagementClosed {engagement, state}` | a terminal record, unless the entry is `end` on `delivered`/`failed`/`partial` |
   | 409 | `CheckinNotReached {engagement, n, kind, when}` | the entry is not reached |
   | 409 | `CheckinDecided {engagement, n, decision, at}` | the entry is already decided |

   **`continue`:**
   - writes `decision`;
   - when the record is `paused` and no reached entry of the cycle is undecided, applies `run-resumed {checkin: n, via}` (`paused → in-progress`);
   - emits `engagement-checkin {…, action: "decided", decision, via}` and, when the state moved, the `engagement` service event;
   - while the project's latest studio event is `studio-closed`, also records `studio-open {project, by: "owner", via: "checkin", engagement, n, reason: "continued at check-in <n> of <id>"}`.

   **`stop`:**
   - writes `decision`;
   - applies `checkin-stop` (to `closed`) unless the record is terminal (an `end` check-in on `delivered`/`failed` keeps its outcome state);
   - emits `engagement-checkin {action: "decided", decision: "stop"}`, `engagement-stopped {engagement, project, n, by: "owner", via, from}` and the `engagement` event.

   The read, the apply and the write run with no await between (the `postInputRoute` rule). Then `afterInput` runs, fire-and-forget.

   **The studio close (run pass).** Once a check-in decided `stop` has no `studio-closed` naming `(engagement, n)`, it is handled on a pass that may run before any link exists:
   - every live linked run is stopped gracefully, reason `stop`, and queued ones are cancelled: step 4 widened to `closed`;
   - when none is live: Jira-bound, `track.py close --directory <root> --engagement <id>` once (`engagement-issue {action: "closed" | "refused", step: "stop"}`);
   - then the line `EndNotice {state: "closed", reason: "engagement <id> stopped at check-in <n> by the owner; the studio is closed"}` with 39.1's route `/app/chat/<id>`, and the notice line;
   - then `studio-closed {project, engagement, n, by: "owner", reason: "stopped at check-in <n> by the owner", notify}`.

8. **The proposals and the open (CAP-22, SPEC design note).**
   - **`stop-after-story {engagement?}`** (39.3) presses as `POST /engagements/{id}/checkin/now {decision: "stop", note: "stop after this story (studio chat)"}`.
     - `now` appends `{n: max+1, kind: "milestone", when: "now", what_to_show: [board, spend], added: "owner", reached_at, shown}`, decides it `stop` at once and answers its `n`.
     - It is allowed in `approved`, `in-progress`, `paused` and `partial`, else `409 IllegalInput {engagement, state, verb: "checkin-stop"}`. `now` with `continue` is `400 SchemaError` ("check-in now takes decision stop").
     - A proposal with no engagement posts it for each of the project's engagements in `in-progress`/`paused` (`GET /engagements?project=`). With none, the page refuses by name: "Nothing to stop: no engagement is running in <project>; a queue run stops through Cancel on its run page".
   - **`open-studio`** presses as the new `POST /studio/open {project, note?}`, cookie + CSRF or bearer.
     - It records `studio-open {project, by: "owner", via, note?, after: <the closed event's seq>}`.
     - It answers `200 {ok: true, project, studio: {state: "open", since}}`.
     - `409 StudioOpen {project, since}` when the latest studio event is not `studio-closed` (none counts as open, as 39.6 reads it); `400 SchemaError` / `MalformedJson`, `403 CsrfRefused`, `404 UnknownProject`.
   - Neither event gates a claim: the hold is the engagement's own.

9. **The notices file (CAP-21, SPEC design note).** `<served root>/_bmad-output/tracking/notices.jsonl`, append-only, UTF-8, one JSON object per line:
   ```
   {v: 1, id, at, source: "run-end" | "cap" | "closeout" | "closeout-refused" | "checkin" | "stopped", project, run_id, engagement, checkin, state, text, route, pr, sent}
   ```
   - Fields: `run_id`/`engagement`/`checkin`/`pr` are null when not applicable; `text` is `noticeText(notice)` as sent; `route` is the relative `/app/…` route; `sent` is `{ok, via?, skipped?, error?}`.
   - `id` per source:
     - `run-end:<run_id>`;
     - `cap:<engagement>:<at>`;
     - `closeout:<engagement>:<run_id>`;
     - `closeout-refused:<engagement>:<run_id>:<at>`;
     - `checkin:<engagement>:<n>`;
     - `stopped:<engagement>:<n>`.
   - The routes:
     - `run-end` → `/app/run/<run_id>`;
     - `cap`, `closeout`, `closeout-refused` and `stopped` → `/app/chat/<engagement>`;
     - `checkin` → `/app/checkin/<engagement>/<n>`.
   - `appendNotice(root, line)` runs after the send answers, whether sent, failed or skipped. It never throws, and it writes only when `<root>/_bmad-output` is a directory (`tracking/` made under it). `recordClaimRefusal` appends nothing.
   - The reader `readNotices(root)` is cached on `(mtimeMs, size)`. A torn line is skipped and the last line of an `id` wins. It answers the newest fifty ids (`INBOX_WINDOW = 50`). The file is never trimmed.

10. **The inbox item (CAP-21, SPEC design note).** Every item gets these fields beside its own: `{id, kind, route, needs_you, at, title}`. The five kinds:

    - **`decision`.** An `inbox.json` item, every field kept.
      - `id`: its key. `route`: `/app/inbox/<KEY>`. `needs_you`: `state === "open"`. `at`: `blocked_since`. `title`: its title, else the reason's first line, else the key.
      - A sweep decision gains `options: [{id: "<DW>", question, options: [{letter, text}], default}]` from `decisions-jira.json`, absent when that file is unreadable.
      - An item carrying `engagement` folds into that engagement's `question` item, else its newest undecided `checkin` item, as `issue: <KEY>`. Otherwise it stays a `decision` routed `/app/chat/<ENG>` (a cap block).
    - **`question`.** One per record in `awaiting-answers` with an unanswered question.
      - `id`: `question:<ENG>`. `route`: `/app/chat/<ENG>`. `needs_you`: true. `at`: the newest `questions` input line's `at`.
      - `title`: `<ENG>: <titleOf(request)>: <k> question(s)`.
      - Also `engagement` and `questions: [{id, text, options, impact?}]`, the open ones.
    - **`checkin`.** One per reached, undecided entry.
      - `id`: `checkin:<ENG>:<n>`. `route`: `/app/checkin/<ENG>/<n>`. `needs_you`: true. `at`: `reached_at`.
      - `title`: `Check-in <n> of <N>: <kind in words>`.
      - Also `engagement, n, of, checkin_kind, when, shown`. A decided entry leaves the inbox; its notice stays.
    - **`notice`.** One per notices window entry.
      - `id`: the line's `id`. `route`: the line's. `needs_you`: false. `at`: the line's. `title`: the text's first line.
      - Also `source, run_id, engagement, pr`.
    - **`message`.** One per line of the last fifty `studio-chat.jsonl` lines (39.3's reader).
      - `id`: `message:<seq>`. `route`: `/app/chat`. `needs_you`: false. `title`: the body's first line, clipped to 120.
      - Also `author, seq`.

    `GET /inbox` answers `{ok, project, generated_at, needs_you: <count>, items}`.

11. **The sort (CAP-21).**
    - The `needs_you` items come first, `at` ascending (longest waiting first).
    - Then the rest, `at` descending (newest first).
    - A null `at` goes last in its group; ties are broken by `id`.
    - The web merges projects by the same rule. The nav count is the number of `needs_you` items.

12. **The web texts (CAP-23).**
    - **Marks.** A tag per kind: `Decision`, `Questions`, `Check-in`, `Notice`, `Message`. A `!` mark on a needs-you row.
    - **The controls in place:**
      - a decision: one option button row per DW id, a note, and Send, which posts `POST /inbox/{key}/reply` with `<DW>: <letter>` lines and the note; a plain block: a note with Send and Done, as the Issue screen;
      - a question: the cards (each option a button) posting `POST /engagements/{id}/input?project= {verb: answer, answers}`;
      - a check-in: Continue and Stop with a note;
      - a notice: Open, which navigates to its route;
      - a message: "Open the chat" (`/app/chat`).
    - **The check-in page:**
      - the heading `Check-in <n> of <N>: <kind in words>`;
      - the kinds in words: `the first landed story`, `story <K> landed`, `the end of the engagement`, `stop after this story`, `spend reached $<X>`, `the playable proof recorded`;
      - "What there is to evaluate:" with the PR link, "The board: /app/board/<project>", "The proof: <path>";
      - the spend line `Spend $<runs> of the $<band> band (cap $<cap>)`.
    - **The consequences:**
      - Stop: "Stop finishes the running story, then closes the studio; the notifier will say so".
      - Continue: "Continue releases the hold: the next story is queued". On `end`: "Continue records the engagement as done; the studio stays open".
    - **The pending and settled states:**
      - "Loading the check-in…" and "Sending your decision…";
      - "Not reached yet: <kind in words>";
      - "Continued at <local time>" or "Stopped at <local time>: the studio closes after the running story";
      - signed out: "Signed out: sign in to answer this check-in", a link to the page's own path (the 401 sign-in carries it as `next`).
    - A refusal is rendered by its error name.

13. **The ledger closures (CAP-23, SPEC design note).** The dev writes each closure by hand, in the in-loop form:
    - DW-48 reads `status: resolved 2026-11-<dd> by Story 39.7 (the Jira-less pause falls out of the check-in route: POST /engagements/{id}/checkin/{n} from the inbox link; Jira continue the fallback)`.
    - DW-178 reads `status: resolved 2026-11-<dd> by Story 39.7 (checkins[] agreed at approval, the check-in page and its route, stop closes the studio; ask = check-ins at every story)`.
    - `ledger-resolve --pr` is not the in-loop writer: it rewrites only `done <date>` and needs the PR number. The landing fills `(thkennedy/tk-studio#N)` as 38.7's rule says.

14. **The serve process.** `api.ts`, `approve.ts`, `engagement.ts`, `inbox.ts`, `hygiene.ts`, `delivery.ts` and the new `notices.ts`/`checkin.ts` run in `serve`. The story's spec says so for the operator's stop-build-restart.
